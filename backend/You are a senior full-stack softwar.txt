You are a senior full-stack software architect, SaaS expert, and ERP specialist.

Your mission is to design and start building a production-ready, multi-tenant SaaS ERP application for optical stores (opticiens), fully adapted to Moroccan business, accounting, and tax regulations.

---

# 🎯 OBJECTIVE

Design a modular, scalable SaaS ERP system for optical shops including:

- Inventory management
- Sales & purchase workflows
- Optical prescription management
- Client management (CRM)
- Payments system
- Moroccan accounting compliance
- VAT (TVA) reporting and declarations
- Analytics and reporting dashboard

---

# 🧩 CORE DOMAIN LOGIC

## 1. Product Types

The system must support two product categories:

### A. Optical lenses (verres correcteurs)
- Fully customized per client
- Require prescription (ordonnance)
- Stored per eye:
  - Right eye (OD)
  - Left eye (OG)
- Parameters:
  - Sphere, Cylinder, Axis, Add, Prism, etc.
- Not part of stock inventory
- Created per sale
- Always linked to client + prescription

### B. Standard products
- Frames (montures)
- Sunglasses
- Accessories

Requirements:
- Managed in stock
- Real-time quantity tracking
- Purchase and sales workflows

---

## 2. Stock Management

- Stock increases via purchases
- Stock decreases via sales
- Every movement must be traceable
- Every movement linked to invoices
- Multi-store support (future-ready)

---

## 3. Sales Workflow (CRITICAL)

Implement full optical workflow:

1. Create quotation (devis)
2. Validate quotation
3. Attach prescription
4. Optional: order lenses
5. Deliver products
6. Generate invoice

Rules:
- Linked to client
- Can include optical + standard products
- Automatic VAT calculation
- Automatic accounting entries generation

---

## 4. Purchases

- Supplier management
- Purchase invoices
- Stock updated after validation only

---

## 5. Client Management

- Client profiles
- History:
  - Prescriptions
  - Purchases
- Multiple prescriptions per client over time
- Optional medical notes

---

## 6. Payments

Support multiple payment methods:
- Cash
- Card
- Credit (partial payments)

Payment statuses:
- Paid
- Partially paid
- Unpaid

---

## 7. Accounting (Moroccan Compliance)

- Moroccan Chart of Accounts (Plan Comptable Marocain)
- Automatic journal entries for:
  - Sales
  - Purchases
  - Payments

### VAT (TVA)
- TVA collectée (sales VAT)
- TVA déductible (purchase VAT)
- VAT declaration reports generation

---

## 8. Reporting & Analytics

- Daily / monthly sales reports
- Top-selling products
- Revenue and margin analysis
- VAT summaries

---

## 9. SaaS Multi-Tenant Architecture

- Each optical store is an isolated tenant
- Secure authentication system
- Role-based access control
